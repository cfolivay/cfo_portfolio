Client project Shopify

420 Philippines

An apparel and accessories brand that also runs a community-awareness program. They wanted one store to handle both: sell products and grow a community.

Visit the live store ↗
  1. Problem

    The brand needed products, a blog and member sign-ups in one place. Running these on separate platforms would split their data and cost more to host.

  2. Approach

    Keep Shopify as the single source of truth. Use built-in features and compatible apps first, and edit theme code only where needed.

  3. What I did
    Storefront customization Liquid theme edits Products & collections Navigation Journal blog Community forms Customer registration App integrations Mobile responsiveness QA testing AdSense preparation
  4. Outcome

    The store is live in production. I still handle fixes and new features as the brand grows.

How I work

Four steps, no surprises

I run every project the way I handled problems in enterprise IT: find the cause, agree on the fix, then document it.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    We talk, and I look at your store or idea. I write down the real problem, not just the visible symptom.

  2. 02

    Plan

    You get a written scope, a timeline and a fixed price before any work starts.

  3. 03

    Build

    You see progress as I go. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    You get plain-language notes on what changed and how to manage it yourself.
